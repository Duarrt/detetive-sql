Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Summary

SQL Detective has no sign-up, no login, no cookies and no ads. Every SQL query runs in your own browser. To know how many people play, we use cookie-free visit counting (GoatCounter), described below.

What stays in your browser

So you don't lose your progress, the game stores in your browser's local storage (localStorage) the cases you solved, your XP, the hints you used, your query drafts and your language choice. This information never leaves your device and never reaches us. To erase it, use the "Reset progress" button at the bottom of the game or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What the server logs

Like any website, the hosting service logs technical access data such as IP address, date and time, page requested and browser type. These logs keep the site running and secure and meet legal obligations. They are not used for profiling or advertising.

Visit counting

We use GoatCounter, an open-source, cookie-free analytics service. On each visit to the game, and the first time a case is solved, your browser sends GoatCounter: the page visited (including the chosen language), the site you came from, the page title, your screen width, and the technical information sent with any web request, such as browser and IP address. According to GoatCounter, it counts unique visits without cookies and without persistently storing personal data. We use these numbers only to know how many people play and how far they get.

To opt out in this browser, open the game with #toggle-goatcounter at the end of the address; repeat it to opt back in.

Third-party resources

Fonts, the database engine (sql.js) and the GoatCounter script itself are served by this site. The only data sent elsewhere is the visit count described above, to duarrt.goatcounter.com.

Your rights
